Calculation and assumptions

For each section, the control median is the median of the two control-lane LOS summaries. Asset-relative LOS is the asset-lane statistic minus that median. This does not erase common movement. Control agreement tolerance and candidate thresholds are analyst-selected screening parameters, not railway limits. LOS does not equal vertical settlement.

What this lab can test

When retained per-pixel phase, mask, coherence and terrain records are supplied, each change reruns the lane medians and matching gates at three concentric radii no larger than the original sampled radius. At each radius, it compares Control A alone, Control B alone, and their median, and reapplies component, subswath, valid-pixel, terrain/radar, and control-disagreement gates. This does not create new offset control polygons or extrapolate outside the uploaded support. Older section-summary inputs only support the three reference scenarios. Results are sensitivity descriptions, not probabilities of damage or failure.

Temporal compatibility and limits

Only usable chainage sections contribute measurements. No-call or missing sections remain listed as gaps. At least three observations must share one reference acquisition and one spatial phase reference, along with compatible geometry, polarization, signed phase convention and effective native support. The measurement date is the secondary acquisition date for a common-reference series. Sample interval is not treated as native spatial resolution. Rates are descriptive only.

Science limits & links

LOS is radar line-of-sight range change, not vertical settlement. Atmospheric delay, decorrelation, unwrapping, geometry, reference choices and spatial support affect interpretation. NISAR L-band provisional data is calibrated but validation coverage varies by site. S-band comparisons are unavailable until compatible products and access are established. No output diagnoses damage or certifies safety.
